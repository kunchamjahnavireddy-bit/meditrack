⚡ Features About the Project

Core capabilities implemented in MediTrack Milestone 1

📝

1. Patient Registration & Auto PAT ID

Automatically assigns unique Patient IDs (PAT001, PAT002, PAT003...). Captures demographics and validates phone numbers, emails, and ages to prevent duplicate records.

🩺

2. Medical Profile Management

Maintains a comprehensive medical file connected to the Patient ID. Stores Blood Group, Known Allergies, Existing Chronic Diseases, Medical History, Medications, Emergency Contact, and Insurance details.

🗓️

3. Smart Appointment Scheduling

Real-time slot picker (09:00 AM – 04:00 PM). Features strict Double-Booking Collision Prevention at both frontend and database levels so a booked slot immediately becomes unavailable.

🔍

4. Unified Patient Directory Search

Hospital staff and doctors can instantly search by Patient ID, Name, or Phone Number to inspect the unified patient file (Demographics + Medical Record + Appointment History).

🩺

5. Doctor Clinical Dashboard

Dedicated physician workspace displaying daily consultation agendas, doctor view switching, and patient record inspection drawers.

📍

6. GPS Geolocation & Google Maps

Interactive location canvas with user-controlled "Use My Current Location" GPS permission button. Stores latitude & longitude coordinates with residential address records.

📖 How to Use MediTrack

Follow this 4-step workflow to manage patient care

01

Step 1: Access Portal & Role

Click Launch Portal and select your role (Patient, Doctor, Receptionist, or Admin). Demo credentials are pre-loaded for easy testing.

02

Step 2: Register Patient

Navigate to Register Patient. The system automatically assigns the next unique Patient ID (e.g. PAT003). Capture GPS location and submit.

03

Step 3: Medical Profile & Booking

Update medical history & allergies under Patient Profile. Go to Appointments, select doctor, date & available time slot to confirm booking.

04

Step 4: Search & Doctor View

Use Search Patients or open Doctor View to review scheduled consultations and inspect the complete patient record.
