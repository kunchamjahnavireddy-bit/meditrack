About MediTrack

Simplifying Patient Care & Medical Management

MediTrack is an Integrated Patient Care Management System designed to simplify patient care, appointments, consultations, medical records, and prescriptions.

Engineered on Node.js, Express.js, and MongoDB Atlas, MediTrack provides a unified, secure platform where patient medical histories, diagnostic reports, and physician prescriptions are accessible in real-time.

Healthcare Services

Our Core Healthcare Services

Comprehensive digital healthcare solutions for modern hospital care

📝

1. Patient Registration & Auto PAT ID

Automatically assigns unique Patient IDs (PAT001, PAT002...) with contact validation and location mapping to prevent duplicate records.

🩺

2. Medical Profiles & Records

Maintains a complete medical file storing Blood Group, Known Allergies, Chronic Diseases, Medical History, Current Medications, and Emergency Contacts.

🗓️

3. Smart Appointment Scheduling

Real-time slot picker with strict double-booking collision prevention so a booked slot immediately becomes unavailable for other patients.

💊

4. Digital Prescriptions & Notes

Physicians issue structured electronic prescriptions (Medicine Name, Dosage, Timing, Duration) and record clinical consultation notes.

📁

5. Direct Lab Reports Upload

Patients can directly upload PDF, JPG, and PNG blood test, X-ray, and scan reports. Authorized doctors view reports associated with their patients.

⚙️

6. Admin Security & Control

Administrative control center for live MongoDB analytics, doctor credential authorization, user account management, permanent deletion, and audit logs.
