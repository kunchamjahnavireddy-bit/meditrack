About MediTrack

Empowering Digital Healthcare Transformation

MediTrack bridges the gap between healthcare providers and patients by centralizing clinical files, real-time appointment scheduling, electronic prescriptions, lab reports, and role-based administrative control.

Engineered with high-reliability Node.js and MongoDB Atlas infrastructure, MediTrack ensures zero data loss, strict role isolation, and real-time access for patients, physicians, and health network administrators.

Healthcare Solutions

⚡ Comprehensive Healthcare Services

Integrated digital workflows engineered for seamless hospital operations

📝

1. Patient Registration & Auto PAT ID

Automatically assigns unique Patient IDs (PAT001, PAT002...). Captures demographics and validates contact details to prevent duplicate medical records.

🩺

2. Medical Profile Management

Maintains a comprehensive medical file connected to the Patient ID. Stores Blood Group, Known Allergies, Existing Chronic Diseases, Medical History, Medications, and Emergency Contacts.

🗓️

3. Smart Appointment Scheduling

Real-time slot picker (09:00 AM – 04:00 PM). Features strict Double-Booking Collision Prevention at both frontend and database levels so a booked slot immediately becomes unavailable.

💊

4. Digital Prescriptions & Notes

Physicians issue structured electronic prescriptions (Medicine Name, Dosage, Timing, Duration, Instructions) and record clinical consultation diagnosis notes.

📁

5. Direct Lab Reports Upload

Patients can directly upload PDF, JPG, and PNG blood test, X-ray, and scan reports. Authorized doctors view reports associated with their patients.

⚙️

6. Admin Control & Security

Administrative control center for live MongoDB analytics, doctor credential authorization, user account management, permanent deletion, and audit logging.
